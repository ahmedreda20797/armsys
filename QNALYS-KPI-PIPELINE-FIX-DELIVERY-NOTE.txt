QNALYS — KPI / REPORTING DATA PIPELINE ROOT-CAUSE FIX
Delivery Note — 2026-09-21
======================================================================

ROOT CAUSE
----------
src/lib/organization/employment-periods.ts → toDayKey() compared
STORED date strings as raw strings. The employee form's documented
hireDate format is DD/MM/YYYY ("28/7/2026" — the form's own
placeholder and parser contract, also produced by the Excel upload),
while every reporting window is ISO ("2026-09-30"). The blind
slice(0,10) kept the day-first string intact and the lexicographic
comparison then fabricated "hired after the period":

    "28/7/2026" > "2026-09-30"   ← position 1: '8' > '0'

→ isEmployeeEligibleForPeriod() returned false
→ NOT_ELIGIBLE_PERIOD in the canonical KPI engine
→ the employee was SILENTLY OMITTED from Monthly/MTD/Historical rows
  (monthly-report.ts `continue` on !eligible), absent from employee
  reports and performance intelligence, despite real September
  evidence (observations / follow-ups / deals).

Affected boundary: employment-periods.ts is THE single lifecycle
source consumed by kpi-framework (employee-result, month builder),
kpi-reporting (monthly/mtd/historical/employee) and
performance-intelligence — one fix propagates everywhere.

SECONDARY CAUSE
---------------
The same blind-slice pattern existed in the archivedAt record-level
guard inside isEmployeeEligibleForPeriod (kpi-framework/
employee-result.ts). Same class of defect, fixed the same way.

AFFECTED POPULATION
-------------------
Every employee whose hireDate was stored day-first AND whose
position-1 day character compared greater than the window's.
Real data (September 2026): 28 of 103 employees carry D/M/YYYY
hire dates; 14 were wrongly NOT_ELIGIBLE_PERIOD. Visibility
accidentally depended on the day's first digit: days 1–2 and
10–20 passed (string prefix "1x/…" < "2026…" when year digits
align), days 3–9 and 21–31 failed.

WHY EMP-075 IS AFFECTED
-----------------------
امنيه السعيد: hireDate stored "28/7/2026" (hired July 28 — employed
throughout September). Raw comparison vs "2026-09-30" = "hired after
September" → NOT_ELIGIBLE_PERIOD → dropped from the monthly report
and her employee report showed "no KPI result" while her evidence
section still showed 3 approved September observations (evidence is
loaded independently of eligibility).

WHY SOME EMPLOYEES STILL APPEARED
---------------------------------
Two populations were immune:
  • ISO-stored hire dates (majority): compared correctly.
  • Closed months with frozen framework results: FROZEN_RESULT is
    returned verbatim and never re-eligibility-checked, so earlier
    closed months kept their results even for affected employees —
    the bug struck OPEN/current months (live path) and legacy
    derived paths. This matches the screenshots: September 2026
    (current) broken while historical data existed.

CORRECT BUSINESS RULE (existing, now actually enforced)
-------------------------------------------------------
"Was the employee employed at ANY point of the period?" (addendum
§10) — employment periods fold from the ledger + fallbackStart and
overlap the window at DAY-KEY granularity. The window is ISO; the
stored hire date may be day-first; both must normalize to the same
canonical YYYY-MM-DD space before comparison. An unparseable stored
value is UNKNOWN presence (fail-open), never a fabricated "hired
after the period".

FIX
---
1. src/lib/organization/employment-periods.ts
   • NEW normalizeDayKey(): ISO day keys/instants pass through;
     D/M/YYYY and DD/MM/YYYY convert to canonical YYYY-MM-DD
     (day-first = the app contract); impossible/garbage → null.
   • employmentOverlapsRange() normalizes every period boundary
     through it before comparing; unparseable start → predates
     known history, unparseable end → still-employed (presence is
     never destroyed by a corrupt string).
2. src/lib/kpi-framework/employee-result.ts
   • The archivedAt record-level guard compares through
     normalizeDayKey (unparseable → guard declines; the ledger
     stays the source of truth).

No stored data was modified. No eligibility was loosened: ISO-only
records behave byte-identically; future hires (ISO or day-first)
stay ineligible for earlier periods.
