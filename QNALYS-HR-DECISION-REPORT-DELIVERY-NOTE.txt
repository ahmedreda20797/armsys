QNALYS FEATURE — HR EMPLOYEE PERFORMANCE & RETENTION DECISION-SUPPORT REPORT
Delivery Note — 2026-09-21
======================================================================

SCOPE
-----
The HR reporting surface is extended from the "final KPI result" view
into a RICHER, deterministic DECISION-SUPPORT projection: each
employee's overall performance is combined with operational behavior
(follow-up discipline, quality/complaint/CAPA signals, stored
attendance, linked deals output, HR deduction aggregates) and the
KPI trend to produce a status WITH the structured measurable factors
that explain it, and a recommended management action.

The report answers: How is this employee performing overall? Is the
problem isolated or repeated? Improving or declining? What measurable
factors explain the status? What management action should be
considered? — NOT merely "what is the KPI percentage?".

CRITICAL SAFETY RULE (PINNED BY TESTS)
--------------------------------------
Decision support, NEVER an automatic employment-decision engine.
- The status vocabulary contains NO termination/fire state:
  STABLE / IMPROVING / NEEDS_COACHING /
  PERFORMANCE_IMPROVEMENT_REVIEW / MANAGEMENT_REVIEW.
- The recommended action is always REVIEW wording ("مراجعة أداء
  رسمية…"), and EVERY status/report/printout carries the fixed
  disclaimer: any final employment decision remains a HUMAN decision
  outside the automated scoring system.
- Tests assert the disclaimer is always attached and that
  termination vocabulary never appears in any payload.

SOURCE-OF-TRUTH DISCIPLINE (nothing modified, nothing duplicated)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
- KPI engine / eligibility / scheme resolution / scores / snapshots /
  reporting loaders / authz engine / scope engine: UNTOUCHED.
- The engine consumes the CANONICAL Phase-3 dataset
  (lib/performance-intelligence — which itself runs the canonical
  KPI report pipeline), the stored HR deduction summaries
  (lib/employee-performance), the canonical risk engine
  (lib/metrics/riskMetrics — computeRisk, weights, bands), the
  canonical follow-up helpers (lib/metrics/followUpMetrics), the
  canonical CAPA helpers (lib/metrics/capaMetrics), the canonical
  repetition detector (lib/repetition-detection), and the configured
  baseline kpiSettings.defaultScore. NO second KPI engine, no
  recalculated scores.

THRESHOLD HONESTY (no invented numbers)
---------------------------------------
- KPI below/above-target judgments use kpiSettings.defaultScore —
  the CONFIGURED baseline the kpi-dashboard already reuses
  (thresholdBasis: 'CONFIGURED').
- Severity gradation reuses the canonical risk bands (11/26/51)
  (thresholdBasis: 'CANONICAL_RISK').
- Signals with NO configured Qnalys threshold (attendance compliance
  minimum, follow-up overdue/completion cutoffs, productivity
  targets) are REPORTED as measured values with
  thresholdBasis: 'CONFIGURATION_REQUIRED' — they never judge and
  never escalate a status. Data-quality notes state this explicitly.

STATUS ENGINE (deterministic, matched-rule, test-pinned)
--------------------------------------------------------
1. No judged negative factors → STABLE (IMPROVING with an upward
   trend).
2. Canonical risk level 'critical' (≥51) → MANAGEMENT_REVIEW.
3. PERFORMANCE_IMPROVEMENT_REVIEW when: MEDIUM+ weakness in ≥2
   DISTINCT dimensions, OR ≥3 consecutive months below the
   configured target, OR ≥3 consecutive declining monthly steps,
   OR canonical risk level 'high' (≥26).
4. NEEDS_COACHING when: exactly ONE weak dimension, or a repeated
   2-step declining trend.
5. LOW-severity concerns are always RECORDED but never escalate;
   reported-only dimensions (attendance/productivity) never count;
   TREND is the KPI dimension's direction, not a separate dimension.
One isolated weak month can therefore never produce a severe status;
escalation requires REPEATED or MULTI-DIMENSION evidence.

HR-SAFE BY SHAPE (server-side, structural)
------------------------------------------
The view models have NO fields for observation text/details,
deduction reasons, complaint/CAPA details, deal customer data,
evidence ids or component math — nothing is sent to HR and hidden
with CSS. Tests assert JSON-level exclusion on the pure reports AND
on the live route responses. HR receives aggregated counts/signals
only. The existing technical reports remain the technical surface;
report-audience profiles are unchanged.

PIPELINE
--------
requireAuth → verifyPermission('reports','view') → audience resolved
(existing report-audience resolver, audit only — the projection is
identical for every admitted caller) → AUTHORIZED employee scope
(resolveEmployeeScopeFromDb, M0.5 — client scope is never trusted)
→ per-employee canonical dataset → decision projection → team
aggregate → JSON. Lifecycle parity with the monthly report: archived
employees appear only on includeArchived, and post-archive
ineligibility remains an engine verdict.
