## File-by-file changes

### 1. `src/lib/metrics/riskMetrics.ts` (NEW) — canonical risk
- `RISK_WEIGHTS` (merged model, recommended option):
  - Delays: `×1`, capped at 15 (noise factor)
  - Absences: `×3`, capped at 30 (severity-weighted, uncapped feel)
  - Quality: `count ×5`, capped at 25
  - HR: `count ×5`, capped at 15
  - Open follow-ups: `×3`, capped at 15
  - High-priority follow-ups: `×5`, capped at 25 (was missing in E360)
  - Critical follow-ups: `×10`, capped at 30
  - Complaints: `×8`, capped at 20 (resolved the 8-vs-5 conflict — use RC's ×8)
  - Repeated issues: `×5`, capped at 15 (was missing in E360)
  - CAPA open/overdue/critical/reopened: **reuse existing `CAPA_RISK_WEIGHTS`/`CAPA_RISK_CAPS`** (already consistent)
- `RISK_LEVELS`: low ≤10 / medium ≤25 / high ≤50 / critical >50, total capped at 100 (the E360 bands — bounded and predictable)
- `computeRisk(input)` → `{ score, level, breakdown }` where `breakdown` is a single canonical shape (one entry per factor with `{count, points}`). This is the ONLY risk calculator in the system.
- All weights/caps/bands exported as named constants for tests.

### 2. `src/lib/metrics/followUpMetrics.ts` (NEW) — overdue on-read
- `ACTIVE_STATUSES = ['open','under_review','under_follow_up']`
- `TERMINAL_STATUSES = ['resolved','closed','cancelled']`
- `isOpenFollowUp(f)` and **`isOverdueFollowUp(f, now=new Date())`** = `f.nextFollowUpDate && new Date(f.nextFollowUpDate) < startOfDay(now) && ACTIVE_STATUSES.includes(f.status)`. (The 12 missed records become overdue immediately.)
- `isDueToday(f, now)`, `isDueThisWeek(f, now)` helpers.

### 3. `src/lib/metrics/capaMetrics.ts` (NEW) — overdue/SLA/effectiveness unification
- `CLOSED_STATUSES = ['closed']`, `TERMINAL_STATUSES = ['closed','rejected']`, `ACTIVE_CAPA_STATUSES` (7-stage list), `SLA_DAYS` map (move the duplicated const from 4 files).
- **`computeOverdueDays(capa, now)`** — single definition: `base = capa.correctiveDueDate ? parse(correctiveDueDate) : parse(createdAt)`; `due = base + slaDays*DAY` (NOT base+sla twice — fixes capa-sla F13); return `>0` if active and now>due. Fixes F4/F5/F13.
- **`isOverdue(capa, now)`** = active && `computeOverdueDays>0`.
- **`calcEffectiveness(cases)`** = `effective / closed × 100` (the reports/capa definition; fixes the 3-denominator F9 issue).
